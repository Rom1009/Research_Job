Trusting Social is an AI Fintech company building credit profiles for underserved consumers across Vietnam, Indonesia, India, and the Philippines, combining alternative data with advanced AI to expand access to credit. Having profiled over 1 billion consumers, the company is now pushing toward 100 million credit lines delivered — grounded throughout in strict data privacy and consumer data rights.
How You’ll Make an Impact
We build the AI that talks to our customers, at the scale of a national consumer portfolio: real conversations, in production, every day. As an MLE on the Data Science team you own how those conversations behave. You design the logic, ship it, watch what it does to the numbers the business is paid on, and change it again the same week.
That closed loop is rare this early in a career. Model, product, and business outcome sit in one pair of hands here, on live traffic rather than a benchmark, with senior data scientists next to you to learn the rigor from. If you want to become a strong data scientist rather than a model trainer, this is the seat where that happens.
What You’ll Do
Agent Design and Ownership
Own the dialogue and decisioning logic behind what the agent says and does, against a business objective rather than a technical one.
Own the conversation end to end, and be able to tell which layer a behavior came from, and fix it there.
Performance, Cost and Quality
Hold latency, cost per conversation and task accuracy together, and move one without giving away the other two.
Build the automated evaluation and monitoring that catches a regression before a customer does.
Iteration and the Frontier
Turn operational and customer feedback into concrete improvements to scripts, prompts and models, and prove the gain with a structured experiment.
Benchmark new models and methods on our own traffic before adopting them, and be willing to say a shiny thing is not worth the migration.
