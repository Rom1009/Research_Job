We are looking for an experienced Middle/Senior AI Engineer to design, build, and deploy artificial intelligence/machine learning solutions into real-world products. The successful candidate will be a key technical contributor, involved from research and model selection through system architecture, production deployment, and performance optimization, while also providing technical leadership and mentoring junior/mid-level team members.
Job Description
• Design and build end-to-end AI/ML systems: from data collection and processing, to model training, evaluation, and production deployment.
• Research, evaluate, and apply appropriate machine learning/deep learning techniques (including large language models - LLMs, computer vision, NLP, recommendation systems, etc.) to solve business problems.
• Build and maintain MLOps pipelines: automate training, testing, deployment (CI/CD), monitoring, and model retraining.
• Optimize performance, latency, scalability, and operating cost of AI systems in production environments.
• Collaborate closely with Product Managers, Data Engineers, Data Scientists, and other stakeholders to translate business requirements into feasible technical solutions.
• Design AI system architecture (APIs, microservices, vector databases, feature stores, etc.) ensuring security, reliability, and maintainability.
• Monitor and evaluate model quality after deployment (model drift, bias, fairness) and propose continuous improvements.
• Provide technical leadership: conduct code/architecture reviews, establish best practices, and coach/mentor junior and mid-level engineers.
• Stay current with emerging AI trends (LLMs, GenAI, agentic AI, etc.) and recommend adoption aligned with product strategy.
• Produce technical documentation, system design documents, and model operating procedures.
