Trusting Social is an AI Fintech company building credit profiles for underserved consumers across Vietnam, Indonesia, India, and the Philippines, combining alternative data with advanced AI to expand access to credit. Having profiled over 1 billion consumers, the company is now pushing toward 100 million credit lines delivered — grounded throughout in strict data privacy and consumer data rights.
How You’ll Make an Impact
The decision engine decides, for every account in a large consumer portfolio, whether we reach out, through which channel, when, and how often. It is a machine that turns models into money, and it runs every day. As an MLE on the Data Science team you build it and you run it.
You will see a model you built change a business number within weeks, on a real portfolio rather than a benchmark, with senior data scientists next to you to learn the rigor from. If you want to become a strong data scientist rather than a model trainer, this is the seat where that happens.
What You’ll Do
Decisioning Ownership
Build the decisioning that picks which accounts we work with, through which channel, when, and how often.
Own a decision end to end, from the signal that triggered it to the outcome it produced.
Models, Pipelines and Reliability
Build the models behind those decisions — propensity, timing, channel response, contactability — and the pipelines that feed them.
Hold recovery, cost and reliability together, and move one without giving away the other two.
Build the monitoring that catches a broken input or a drifted model before the business notices.
Evidence and the Frontier
Backtest a change before it ships and measure it on live traffic after, on the metric the business is actually paid on.
Benchmark new methods on our own portfolios and our own metrics before adopting them.

How You Work
You build models and pipelines, and you use AI to get more of it done — writing and reviewing code, exploring data, running more variants than you could by hand — while judging every output on evidence before it reaches a live decision.
You experiment proactively instead of waiting to be told what to build — you take a hypothesis to the data, then bring the result to the table.
You move fluidly between modeling, data engineering and business context, coordinating across product and operations without needing a handoff document.
You treat every failed experiment as a learning input and keep sharpening your craft without being told to.
