About Knorex
Founded in 2009, Knorex (https://knorex.com) is a technology company that provides programmatic advertising products and solutions to marketers to connect in real-time to their desired audience worldwide. Through its flagship universal marketing platform, Knorex XPO unifies the connectivity to each of the major marketing channels including social media, search, connected TVs/OTTs, video, audio, display/native, and email, in one place, while simplifying execution and optimization across these channels through AI automation. Knorex operates across the US and APAC.
Job Description
We are looking for a motivated Junior Software Engineer to join our Real-Time Bidding (RTB) team and help develop and maintain our high-performance bidding engine.
Our RTB system processes bid requests from advertising exchanges in real time, making bidding decisions under strict latency requirements. You will work with experienced engineers to build reliable, scalable, and efficient backend systems while learning about programmatic advertising and real-time distributed systems.
This role is suitable for fresh graduates or engineers with up to one year of experience who have a strong foundation in programming, enjoy solving technical problems, and are eager to grow as backend engineers.

Develop, test, and maintain backend services and components of the RTB bidding engine
Implement new features and improve existing functionality under senior engineers' guidance
Write clean, efficient, maintainable, and well-tested code

Contribute to performance optimization to improve the bidding engine's latency, throughput, and resource efficiency
Integrate with external advertising platforms and internal services through APIs and messaging systems
Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and engineering best practices
Collaborate with team members to deliver features and improvements on time
Learn and apply new technologies, tools, and techniques to improve software quality and development productivity
